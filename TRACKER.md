## Constraint register

Beyond the constitution's invariants (instrument law for any simulated-family or scoring prompt; family-instrument facts staying consistent with paper 02's evidence walk), the gap register imposes these standing rules on the draft while gaps remain open:

- **No FAD or SCORE-15 numeric benchmark may be stated as established** until the verified family table exists (item 01).
- **Neither branch may be declared** — full benchmark or narrowed construct-architecture — until the licensing outcome is recorded (item 02). If authorized instrument text is not obtained, the title and the benchmark claims narrow to the construct-architecture branch; that is already stated in the title footnote.
- **No result shell in §5 may be replaced with content** until the rung-2 calibration actually runs.
- **Novelty of the multi-informant LLM-psychometrics intersection may not be claimed** without the sweep in item 07.
- **Cross-informant divergence is reported only decomposed** into a perception-attributable share and a response-distortion-attributable share — never as an undecomposed number. This is a design commitment of the paper itself, not a gap.
- **Leads are not sources.** Every lead listed in the gap register is unverified by construction and may not be cited from that list; new sources enter `references.bib` only after primary-source verification.
